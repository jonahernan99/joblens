# Machine Learning Engineer - Toronto, ON (Hybrid)

_Fictional job posting used for demonstration purposes._

## About the Role

Our applied AI team builds document intelligence products for Canadian
financial institutions. You will design, train and deploy NLP models and
LLM-powered features that process millions of documents per month.

## What You'll Do

- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over internal documents
- Train and evaluate NLP models with PyTorch and Hugging Face
- Ship models as REST APIs using FastAPI and Docker
- Partner with data engineering on Airflow data pipelines

## Requirements

- 2+ years of professional experience with Python
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals and statistics
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, embeddings and vector databases
- Strong SQL skills and experience with PostgreSQL
- Experience deploying workloads on AWS
- Comfortable with Git, code reviews and unit testing

## Nice to Have

- Experience with Kubernetes and Terraform
- Exposure to MLOps tooling such as MLflow
- Familiarity with LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Bilingual (English/French) is an asset
